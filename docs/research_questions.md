# Research Questions

## Primary question

How is Canada's electricity system changing, and where are the biggest opportunities and challenges in the transition toward cleaner generation?

## Candidate sub-questions

These are working questions, not fixed hypotheses.

1. How has Canada's electricity generation mix changed over time?
2. How do electricity systems differ across provinces and territories?
3. Which generation technologies are gaining or losing share, and where?
4. Which provinces have experienced the largest changes in carbon-intensive generation?
5. How important are electricity imports, exports, and interprovincial flows to system operation?
6. What evidence exists of reliability or resilience constraints during the transition?
7. Where could new demand from electrification create the greatest system pressure or opportunity?
8. Which transition challenges appear national, and which are fundamentally provincial?

## Hypothesis protocol

Candidate hypotheses will be proposed only after relevant data is reviewed. For each hypothesis, the project should document:

- Why it is plausible
- Which data can test it
- What result would support it
- What result would weaken or contradict it
- Important confounding factors or limitations
