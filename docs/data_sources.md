## Source acceptance criteria

A dataset should generally satisfy most of the following:

- Authoritative or well-documented source
- Sufficient geographic detail
- Sufficient time coverage
- Stable definitions across time
- Clear units and methodology
- Reproducible access or downloadable raw data
- Material contribution to a research question
